== Overview

The LZA Universal Configuration provides a baseline foundation that serves organizations worldwide. While the core configuration incorporates security best practices and Well-Architected principles applicable across all industries, specific regions and industries often have unique regulatory, compliance, and operational requirements.

This section provides specialized guidance developed in coordination with AWS teams representing customers from different regions and industries. Each guide builds upon the Universal Configuration baseline with targeted enhancements to help you meet your specific requirements faster.

Review the guidance for your region or industry before deployment to understand specific considerations, configuration requirements, and compliance mappings that apply to your environment.

== Supported Regions and Industries

=== US Federal

For US government agencies, contractors, and organizations handling sensitive federal workloads. Covers AWS GovCloud (US) deployment, federal compliance frameworks, and enhanced security controls.

link:us-federal/index.adoc[View US Federal Guidance →]

=== AWS European Sovereign Cloud

For European organizations with data sovereignty requirements deploying workloads in the AWS European Sovereign Cloud partition. Covers EUSC service availability, partition-specific configuration, and deployment guidance.

link:european-sovereign-cloud/index.adoc[View European Sovereign Cloud Guidance →]

=== Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA)

For organizations in Saudi Arabia aligning with the National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA) frameworks (ECC-2:2024, CCC-2:2024) and the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL). Covers control mapping, region restriction, data residency, identity governance, data classification, and extended backup retention on top of the Universal Configuration baseline.
